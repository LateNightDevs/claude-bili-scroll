# find-something rules

What Claude looks for and how it talks. Edit freely, or ask Claude: "change my find-something rules: …".
While the line above is here, Claude asks you a few questions on the next run and fills this in.

## What I'm into

- **Games:** (e.g. story RPGs, Souls-likes, 鸣潮)
- **Anime / manga:** (e.g. romance, shonen, new seasons)
- **Tech / other:** (e.g. AI tools, databases)

## Where to look

- **Bilibili:** my home feed first (`bili_recommend`, 2–3 batches); if it's thin, rankings (3-day) for `game`, `douga`, `technology`, `knowledge`, plus 每周必看, trending searches, and keyword search for:
  - (keywords, one per line, e.g. 鸣潮, 艾尔登法环)
- **Web search** for news on the topics above.

## Date range

Anything posted this year.

## View bar

- At least **10万 views**, or at least **1万 views with likes ≥ 3% of views**.
- Rankings and 每周必看 are already filtered by Bilibili.
- A smaller video only as a marked "hidden gem".
- Keyword search: sort by 综合 (`totalrank`) or 播放 (`click`).

## Caps (keep the round cheap)

- Subtitles for **at most 6 videos** per round, **first ~3000 characters** each.
- Stop at 2–3 good finds.
- Skip the round if my usage limit is above ~80%.

## How many

2 or 3 finds per round. Fewer is fine if nothing's worth it.

## Avoid

- Spoilers.
- Ads, sales posts and clickbait.

## How to talk

Like a friend who saw the news: a few plain lines, no lists. Keep each find in its source's language; if most finds are Chinese, open in Chinese.
